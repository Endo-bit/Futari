A GUIDED FIRST RUN
Instead of explaining Futari, it now writes a page with you. Each step lights up the real control on the real screen and waits until you have actually done it — by the end you have written today and opened a page with someone.

TRY THE SHARED DIARY BEFORE YOU PAIR
The part Futari is really for — writing apart, then opening together — no longer waits on talking someone into installing an app. Write your page opposite a stand-in partner, reveal the two side by side, and send a reply back. Nothing from the demo is saved.

ONE TAP TO PAIR
Invites are a link now. Your partner taps it and you are paired — no six-character code to read out, no hunting through Settings.

REMINDERS AT THE TIME YOU CHOSE
The evening reminder now arrives at your time, in your time zone; it used to ignore the hour you picked. Answering only the day's prompt counts as having written, so it will not nudge you about a page you have already filled in.

THREE MONTHS OF PROMPTS
Six times as many prompts to write to, and three times as many couple questions. Writing on your own used to come back around to the same question inside a fortnight.
